Questions

Is this actually different from AmbitionBox or Glassdoor?

Yes. AmbitionBox aggregates self-reported numbers that are often inflated. Our salary data comes from anonymous submissions with context — company type, city, tech stack, and verified by community pattern checks. We show medians, not modes.

Can I cancel the Pro subscription anytime?

Yes. Email us at hello@careerreality.in before renewal. There are no lock-ins. Monthly billing, cancel before the 30-day cycle ends.

What is the Exit Checklist and how is it delivered?

After completing the Resignation Risk Analyzer, you can purchase a personalized 7-day exit checklist. It includes action items specific to your company type, notice period, and situation. Delivered via email within 5 minutes of payment confirmed.

Is my payment secure?

Payments are processed by Razorpay — India's largest payment gateway. We never see or store your card details. Razorpay is PCI-DSS compliant.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes for the ₹99 checklist if you contact us within 24 hours and it wasn't opened. Pro subscriptions are refunded if cancelled within 3 days of renewal.
