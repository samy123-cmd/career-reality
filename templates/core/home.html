Career Reality India publishes data-backed analysis of the Indian tech job market. Every page covers the trade-offs you need to plan your career — not the optimistic framing most career content uses to sell courses.

Who it is for: software engineers, product managers, and data professionals at any stage who want to understand what Indian tech salaries actually look like, how much competition has increased, when job-switching makes financial sense, and which skills are losing market value faster than hiring trends suggest.

All salary ranges, difficulty estimates, and market signals are reviewed monthly. Sources and review dates are listed on every page. We take corrections publicly.

EDITORIAL BASELINE

Career Reality is an editorial publication, not a lead-gen funnel. We prioritize:

  • Original analysis over rewritten trend content.
  • Source-backed salary and hiring claims with review dates.
  • Transparent corrections and monthly content refresh cycles.
