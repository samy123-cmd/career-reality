Topic Clusters

This is our editorial map. If the internet gives you random career advice, this page gives you structure — which articles to read first, which tools to use after, and which claims to ignore.

How To Use This Page

  1. Start with a pillar page for baseline reality (salary bands, risk signals, or role trade-offs).
  2. Move into role-specific or risk-specific supporting articles — read at least two before deciding.
  3. Use tools only after you understand context: CTC math, resignation risk, or layoff signals.
  4. Return here when a LinkedIn post or recruiter pitch pulls you into a new rabbit hole.

Why We Cluster Content

Indian tech careers fail less from lack of effort and more from mismatched advice. A fresher reading Staff-engineer LinkedIn posts, a 7-year services engineer chasing MBA prestige, and a PM evaluating GCC offers do not need the same article. Topic clusters keep signals local to the decision you are actually making: compensation quality, exit timing, role depth, or market risk.

Each cluster below links a pillar (baseline framing) to supporting pages (deeper trade-offs) and the free tools that quantify the claim. We add pages only when they change a decision — not to inflate article counts for search or AdSense optics.

Recommended Reading Paths

If you are evaluating an offer: start with Salary Reality, decode the letter with the CTC Calculator, then read what 20 LPA actually feels like and joining-bonus clawbacks.

If you are thinking of resigning: run the Resignation Risk Analyzer, check Layoff Radar for your employer, then read relieving-letter traps and layoff recovery timelines.

If your title stopped matching your growth: pick your role cluster under Role Topics, then read the plateau / IC-vs-manager pieces before you pay for another course or MBA. Upskilling without scope evidence is a common Indian-market trap — see why upskilling stops working.

Quality Rule
