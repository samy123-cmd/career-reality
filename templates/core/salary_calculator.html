What This Calculator Covers

Standard Indian CTC structure: variable pay, employer PF, gratuity, employee PF, professional tax, and income tax under both regimes. It selects the lower tax regime by default.

Assumptions and Limits
  • Basic salary assumed as 40 percent of fixed pay.
  • Employer PF capped at the standard monthly ceiling when basic exceeds 15k.
  • Only PF is considered for 80C; other deductions are not included.
  • Stock grants, ESOPs, and benefits are excluded.
Common Scenarios
  • Service companies often have higher variable pay and lower fixed take-home.
  • Startups may show higher fixed pay but smaller employer PF contributions.
  • Metro HRA benefits apply only when rent is paid and declared.
FAQ — CTC to in-hand salary calculator India

How do I calculate CTC to in-hand salary in India? Start with annual CTC, subtract employer PF and gratuity, remove unpaid variable, then estimate tax under new vs old regime. Divide what’s left by 12 for monthly in-hand. This free In-Hand Salary Calculator follows that 2026 structure.

Why is my in-hand salary lower than my CTC? CTC packs employer PF, gratuity, insurance, and variable that are not monthly cash. A “20 LPA” offer often lands closer to ₹1.1–1.4L in-hand after tax — decode before you accept.

Is this CTC calculator exact for every Indian company? It is a strong estimate for common IT/product structures. Payslips still vary with HRA proofs, special allowances, and ESOP tax. Compare offers here, then verify the annexure.

New or old tax regime? Most mid-level offers favour the new regime unless HRA and deductions are large. Run both paths — metro rent in Bengaluru/Hyderabad can flip the winner.

Edge Cases We Do Not Model Yet
  • NPS employer contribution and other retirement benefits.
  • Special allowances tied to location or shift timing.
  • ESOPs, stock grants, and retention bonuses.
  • Insurance premiums and reimbursement limits.
