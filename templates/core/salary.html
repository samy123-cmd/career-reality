How to read this

Editorial band is the typical offer span for most Indian employers in that cluster — services majors, mid-market product, and non-unicorn startups. It is not a statistical P25–P90 unless a verified sample set is published separately.

Hype is the ceiling influencers and bootcamps advertise. Plan on the band; treat the hype as lottery.

Market note ({% now "F Y" %}): GCC premiums persist with RTO; Staff+ slots stay scarce; AI premiums remain narrow.
