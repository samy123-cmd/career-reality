Revenue Model

We make money. Transparently. If editorial work has no business model, it eventually gets replaced by something with one. We prefer a model that does not require pretending every trend is revolutionary.

Principles

  • Editorial first, monetization second.
  • Diversified income so no single partner can bend coverage.
  • Clear labeling for all commercial placements.

Current Streams

  • Display advertising: policy-compliant, separated from editorial conclusions.
  • Newsletter growth: reader channel for recurring analysis and updates.
  • Research outputs: paid deep dives built from aggregated market signals.

What We Do Not Sell

  • Guaranteed career outcomes.
  • Positive coverage as a service.
  • Hidden affiliate recommendations dressed as neutral advice.

Commercial Constraints

  • No sponsor can approve, delay, or edit independent editorial content.
  • Sponsored units are disclosed and separated from ranking systems.
  • Correction policy applies equally everywhere.

Roadmap

  • Quarterly index report with downloadable benchmark tables.
  • Role-level salary and switching-risk packs for subscribers.
  • More tooling that improves planning quality, not panic quality.

Short Version

If revenue threatens editorial integrity, we cut the revenue path. Bad money is expensive money.
